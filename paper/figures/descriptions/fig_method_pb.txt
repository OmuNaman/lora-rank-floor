LoRA Adaptation of a Small Transformer Encoder (DistilBERT)
IMPORTANT: Pure white background (#FFFFFF). No gray tint, no gradient. Clean, flat, academic.

== CONTEXT ==
A schematic of how LoRA fine-tunes a small encoder for text classification. The pretrained DistilBERT backbone is frozen; a small low-rank update is added to the attention projections; a small classification head is trained. The figure also shows the two bracketing baselines: a rank-0 probe (head only) and full fine-tuning (everything trainable).

== LAYOUT ==
Left-to-right horizontal flow in three zones. Zone 1 (left): input. Zone 2 (middle): the frozen encoder stack. Zone 3 (right, enlarged "zoom" callout): one attention weight matrix showing the frozen weight plus the trainable low-rank branch. Far right: the trainable classification head and the output label. A thin labelled bracket strip runs along the bottom.
